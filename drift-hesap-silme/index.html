Delete account

Delete your Drift account.

You can delete your account and its data yourself, in the app, in a few taps. If you cannot, e-mail us and we will do it.

In the app

  1. Open Drift and go to Upload.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If something fails halfway, tap Delete account again; running it twice is safe. You are signed out only when the deletion has finished.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, birth year, sign-in record.
  • Your likes and your blocks (both directions) and your appeals.
  • Your uploads: they are taken down at once, and other phones remove their copies the next time they connect. The files then follow the usual schedule (up to 90 days in a private archive, then deleted).
  • Your ad reward confirmations (transaction ID and time of each ad that earned an upload).
  • The Pro purchase link: the one-way digest of your Google Play purchase token, the product and the Pro end date. The purchase itself stays with your Google account.
  • The reports you sent stay, but no longer point to you.

What is kept

  • If your account had strikes or was banned, or had used its free first upload (so it is not given again): a one-way fingerprint of your Google account and the strike count, for 1 year, so a closed account cannot simply return. It cannot be turned back into your e-mail or name.
  • Moderation log entries and copyright notices about your uploads, for 1 year.
  • Reports other people made about your profile: resolved ones are deleted 1 year after they are handled; open ones are kept until a moderator handles them.
  • For each of your removed or rejected uploads, once its files are deleted: its title, tags and a content fingerprint, so the same file can't be uploaded again. They are no longer linked to you.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Cannot use the app?

E-mail main@foastudios.com from the address linked to your Google sign-in, with the subject "Delete my Drift account" and your @username. We will reply to confirm it is you, delete the account, and tell you when it is done.

You do not need an account to use Drift without uploading. Without an account, Drift keeps no profile of you.

Hesap silme (Türkçe özet)

Uygulamada: Drift → Upload → Account → Delete account ve onaylayın. Yarıda kalırsa yeniden basabilirsiniz.

Silinenler: profiliniz, beğenileriniz, engellemeleriniz, itirazlarınız, reklam ödülü onaylarınız ve Pro satın alma bağlantınız (belirteç özeti, ürün, bitiş tarihi); yüklemeleriniz hemen kaldırılır ve diğer telefonlardaki kopyalar silinir. Gönderdiğiniz şikâyetler kalır ama size bağlı olmaz.

Tutulanlar: uyarısı ya da yasağı olan ya da ilk ücretsiz yüklemesini kullanmış (yeniden verilmesin diye) hesaplarda Google hesabının tek yönlü parmak izi ve uyarı sayısı 1 yıl; yüklemelerinizle ilgili moderasyon kayıtları ve telif bildirimleri 1 yıl; profiliniz hakkındaki şikâyetler sonuçlandıktan 1 yıl sonra silinir (açık olanlar işlenene dek kalır); kaldırılan ya da reddedilen yüklemelerinizin dosyaları silindikten sonra aynı dosya yeniden yüklenemesin diye başlık, etiketler ve içerik parmak izi kalır, size bağlı olmaz.

Uygulamayı kullanamıyorsanız: main@foastudios.com adresine, Google girişinizle bağlı adresten "Delete my Drift account" konusuyla ve kullanıcı adınızla yazın.
