Privacy Policy

What Drift keeps, and for how long.

You can use Drift without an account, and then it keeps no profile of you. An account is only for uploading, liking, blocking and appealing in the community library. This page says what that account stores and who helps us run it.

Without an account

Browsing the library, previewing, saving wallpapers to your phone, setting them as your wallpaper and sending a report need no account. Your own imports (a GIF, photo or video you pick with Android's file picker) are copied to Drift's private storage on your phone and stay there. If you turn on the optional Parallax effect, Drift reads the accelerometer in real time for the effect only; these readings are never stored or sent. Drift Pro is bought and handled by Google Play; Drift only asks Google Play whether Pro is active. The purchase token is sent to Google Play to confirm Pro and is not stored. Reports and copyright notices sent without an account are stored (with no link to an account), because we need them to act on them.

With an account

Signing in uses your Google account. This is what we then store:

  • Account: your Google e-mail address and name (kept by our sign-in service), the username you pick (public) and your birth year (private, only to check you are 13 or older).
  • What you add: the wallpapers you upload with their title, tags and source note, your likes, the people you block, your appeals, and the reports you send (a report with no account is stored without any account link).
  • Pro check: the Google Play purchase token is sent to Google Play to confirm Pro; it is not stored.
  • Copyright notices sent by rights holders: the name, e-mail address and reasoning they give us.

We use this to run the library, review uploads, handle reports and notices, and confirm Pro. We do not sell data, show ads or run analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Drift does not read or store a device ID or an advertising ID.

Technical data and your IP address

To stop abuse (for example a flood of reports from one network) the server limits how many reports and copyright notices one person can send per day. For people without an account that limit is counted per network address. We don't store the raw IP address in our database: only a daily one-way digest used for abuse limits is kept, and each such row is deleted after 7 days. Our hosting providers (Supabase, Cloudflare) may keep standard request logs for a short time. Download counts are kept as a plain total with no link to anyone.

Who helps us run it

These services process data for us, and only for running Drift:

  • Supabase (EU region): accounts, sign-in and the database.
  • Cloudflare: storage (R2) and queues for uploaded files, and delivery of wallpapers.
  • Google Cloud (EU region): the worker that converts uploads, and Cloud Vision, which scans frames for unsafe content.
  • Google Play: purchases, and its billing component's own diagnostics under Google's privacy policy.

Some of these providers (Google Play, Cloudflare) may process data outside the EU under their standard safeguards. Public wallpapers and the username of the uploader are visible to everyone.

Data controller: FOA Studios, main@foastudios.com

Your rights and deleting your account

You can see, correct and delete your data. The quickest way is in the app: Upload → Account → Delete account. That removes your profile, likes, blocks and appeals, takes your wallpapers down and removes them from other phones. If you cannot use the app, ask by e-mail.

Also kept after deletion (besides the wallpaper records above, which no longer point to you): if your account had strikes or was banned, a one-way fingerprint of your Google account is kept for 1 year together with the strike count, so a closed account cannot simply come back. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into your e-mail or name.

Under the EU GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you also have the right to ask for access, correction, deletion and restriction, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to main@foastudios.com.

Age

You must be 13 or older to have an account, upload, like, block or appeal. Browsing needs no account. We do not knowingly keep an account of anyone under 13; if you think we do, write to us and we will delete it.

Rules, copyright and changes

What may be shared is in the community rules. Rights holders can send a copyright notice. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions: main@foastudios.com

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe özet)

Hesapsız kullanımda Drift sizin hakkınızda bir profil tutmaz. Hesapsız gönderilen şikâyetler ve telif bildirimleri (bir hesaba bağlanmadan) saklanır. Hesap yalnız topluluk kütüphanesinde yükleme, beğenme, engelleme ve itiraz için gerekir.

Hesapla saklananlar: Google e-posta ve adınız (giriş hizmetimizde), seçtiğiniz kullanıcı adı (herkese açık), doğum yılınız (gizli, yalnız 13 yaşını doğrulamak için), yüklediğiniz duvar kağıtları, beğeniler, engellemeler, itirazlar, gönderdiğiniz şikâyetler ve Pro doğrulaması için satın alma belirteci (Google Play'e gönderilir, saklanmaz). Reklam, analitik, izleme ya da çökme raporu SDK'sı yoktur; cihaz kimliği ve reklam kimliği toplanmaz; veri satılmaz.

IP adresi: kötüye kullanımı sınırlamak için günlük şikâyet ve telif bildirimi sayısı sayılır. Ham IP adresini veritabanımızda saklamayız; yalnız kötüye kullanım sınırı için günlük tek yönlü bir özet tutulur ve 7 gün sonra silinir. Barındırma sağlayıcılarımız (Supabase, Cloudflare) kısa süre standart istek günlüklerini tutabilir.

Saklama: onaylanan yüklemenin ham dosyası onaydan 7 gün sonra silinir; reddedilen/kaldırılan içeriğin dosyaları itiraz için 90 gün arşivde tutulur, dosyaları 90 gün sonra silinir; aynı dosya yeniden yüklenemesin diye kaydın başlığı, etiketleri ve içerik parmak izi kalır (hesap silinince kaydın yükleyene bağlantısı kaldırılır); moderasyon kaydı ve telif bildirimleri 1 yıl tutulur; sonuçlanmış şikâyetler işlendikten 1 yıl sonra silinir, açık olanlar bir moderatör işleyene dek kalır; hesabınızı silerseniz profil, beğeni, engelleme ve itirazlarınız silinir.

Silinmiş hesapta kalan: uyarısı ya da yasağı olan bir hesap silinirse, kapatılan hesabın geri dönememesi için Google hesabının tek yönlü parmak izi ve uyarı sayısı 1 yıl tutulur. Bu izden e-postanız ya da adınız geri çıkarılamaz.
