Drift Live Wallpapers

Privacy Policy

Your wallpapers stay on your phone.

Drift collects no personal data and its own code sends nothing off your device. Your wallpapers, imports and settings stay on your phone. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking

What the app touches

Two things on your device, and your Pro status from Google Play:

Photos, GIFs & videos you import

When you tap Add your own, Android’s system file picker lets you choose a GIF, image or video. Drift copies only the file you pick into its own private storage to use as a wallpaper. It never scans or reads your gallery. Imported files stay on your device and are deleted when you remove them in the app or uninstall it.

Motion sensor (accelerometer)

If you turn on the optional Parallax effect, Drift reads the accelerometer to shift the wallpaper as you tilt your phone. This is used only for the visual effect, in real time, and is never stored or transmitted.

Purchases (Drift Pro)

Drift Pro subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Google Play. Drift never sees your payment details. It only asks Google Play whether Pro is active on your account and stores that yes/no answer on your device.

You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

What it never collects or uses

  • No account, name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • No location.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No uploads by Drift. The app holds an internet permission only because Google Play’s billing component (used for Drift Pro) requires it; that component may send purchase diagnostics to Google under Google’s privacy policy. Drift’s own code never uses the network.
  • Drift itself shares nothing with any third party.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
